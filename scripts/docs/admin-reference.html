01 Chapter one · Roles

Nine roles, one ladder.

Your role is worked out per place. You can be an Event Manager in one organization and a Viewer in another.

Super Admin · runs the platform
Enterprise AdminEnterprise Manager
Org AdminEvent Manager
CoordinatorFinance ManagerViewer
User · every attendee
Super AdminRuns the platform. Sees every enterprise, organization, user, payment and audit entry. The only role that can open /super-admin.
Enterprise AdminOwns an enterprise, such as a whole university. Creates and removes its organizations, handles billing, sees analytics across them.
Enterprise ManagerLike the Enterprise Admin, but can't touch billing or delete the enterprise. Can view payments, not refund them.
Org AdminFull control of one organization: events, members, payments and refunds, coupons, certificates, the public website.
Event ManagerCreates and runs events, edits the registration form, approves registrations, sends messages and works the door. No payments, no members.
CoordinatorEvent-day help. Checks people in and sees who's registered. Read-only everywhere else.
Finance ManagerPayments, refunds, payment exports and reports, coupons and analytics. Can see events and registrations but can't change them.
ViewerSees events, registrations, payments, check-ins and basic analytics. Changes nothing. Good for auditors.
UserEvery attendee. Gets /dashboard and the public site, and no admin pages.
02 Chapter two · Admin pages

Every page, and who uses it.

Organization, enterprise and platform, in that order.

Organization admin

/admin/orgHome: counters, recent activity, upcoming events.
/admin/org/eventsAll events. Each row's menu: View details, Edit, Publish, Registrations, Check-in, Duplicate, plus Cancel or Resubmit for approval.
/admin/org/events/createThe six-step event wizard. /admin/org/events/[id]/edit opens the same wizard on an existing event.
/admin/org/events/[id]/registrationsSearch, filter by status (including held and waitlisted), approve or reject, add someone by hand, export CSV.
/admin/org/events/[id]/check-inQR scanner, manual search, live counts and undo. /admin/org/check-in picks the event first.
/admin/org/events/[id]/analyticsNumbers for one event.
/admin/org/registrationsRegistrations across every event in the organization.
/admin/org/paymentsEvery payment. Refund on a row, Refund all for a cancelled event, CSV export. Also /payouts and /reports.
/admin/org/membersInvite, change roles, remove. An invite to an existing user takes effect at once. A new email joins when it's verified.
/admin/org/communicationsEmails, drafts, announcements.
/admin/org/certificatesTemplates and issuing certificates after an event.
/admin/org/couponsDiscount codes: percent or fixed, usage limits, dates, which events.
/admin/org/analyticsReports for the whole organization.
/admin/org/audit-logsEvery change: who, what, when, from where.
/admin/org/websiteThe public site. Also /editor, /templates, /content, /modules, /media and /preview.
/admin/org/billingThe organization's plan and invoices.
/admin/org/settingsName, branding, payment settings. /settings/developers holds API keys and webhooks for /api/v1.

Enterprise admin

/admin/enterpriseHome across all organizations.
/admin/enterprise/organizationsCreate, edit and deactivate organizations.
/admin/enterprise/eventsEvery event in the enterprise. With Require event approval switched on, approve or reject here, one at a time or in bulk.
/admin/enterprise/registrationsRegistrations across the enterprise, with CSV export.
/admin/enterprise/paymentsPayments and revenue across organizations, with CSV export.
/admin/enterprise/membersEnterprise-level admins and managers.
/admin/enterprise/analyticsReports across the enterprise.
/admin/enterprise/audit-logsThe enterprise audit trail, with CSV export.
/admin/enterprise/brandingLogo and colours.
/admin/enterprise/billingPlan and invoices. Enterprise Admin only.
/admin/enterprise/settingsDefaults, domain, ownership transfer.

Platform (Super Admin)

/super-adminPlatform numbers.
/super-admin/enterprisesVerify, deactivate, delete, transfer ownership.
/super-admin/organizationsEvery organization.
/super-admin/eventsEvery event.
/super-admin/usersBan or unban, change role.
/super-admin/subscriptionsPlans and who's on them.
/super-admin/analyticsPlatform-wide numbers.
/super-admin/auditThe platform audit trail.
/super-admin/messagesThe contact-form inbox.

04 Chapter four · Walkthroughs

Four jobs, step by step.

The roles that actually spend their day in the admin area.

Org Admin  Run an event from start to finish

  1. /admin/org/events, then Create Event. Go through the six steps.
  2. On the last step, Preview opens the public page with a "Preview mode" bar. Check it, then publish. If your enterprise approves events first, it waits as Pending approval.
  3. /admin/org/coupons: add an early-bird code for this event.
  4. /admin/org/communications: email the link to your members or a list.
  5. Registrations appear under the event as they come in. Approve them in bulk if the event needs approval.
  6. On the day, open check-in on a phone and scan.
  7. Afterwards, issue certificates from /admin/org/certificates and look at the event's analytics.

Finance Manager  Give a refund

  1. /admin/org/payments: search by payment number or email.
  2. Press Refund on the row. A "Refund PAY-…" box opens.
  3. Leave the full amount or enter less, add a reason, confirm.
  4. Razorpay payments are refunded through Razorpay. Cash payments are just recorded. A full refund also cancels the registration and gives back the seat and the coupon use. Everything is logged.

Cancelled an event?

Use Refund all on the payments page. It refunds every payment for that event in one go.

Coordinator  Work the door

  1. Open /admin/org/events/[id]/check-in on a phone and allow the camera.
  2. Scan each QR. A green confirmation appears and the count goes up.
  3. QR won't read? Search by name, email or registration number and check the person in by hand.
  4. Checked in the wrong person? Undo it from the same screen.

Super Admin  Deal with a reported user

  1. /super-admin/users: search by email and open the user.
  2. Look at their role, organizations, registrations and payments.
  3. If needed, Ban them (reversible with Unban) or change their role.
  4. /super-admin/audit: filter by that user to see what they did.

A2 Appendix · Live server

Settings for a live server.

The environment variables. Full setup steps are in DEPLOY.md and ZOHO-DEPLOY.md.

Required

DATABASE_URLThe PostgreSQL connection string.
NEXTAUTH_SECRET32+ random characters. Signs sign-in sessions.
NEXTAUTH_URL, NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URLThe site's public address. The second one is built into the app, so rebuild after changing it.
TICKET_SECRET32+ random characters. Signs every ticket QR. Never change it after launch, or every existing ticket stops scanning.
CRON_SECRETProtects the timed jobs (releasing unpaid seats every 5 minutes, hourly reminders).
EMAIL_FROMSender address. In production the app won't send mail without it.

Email, payments, files

RESEND_API_KEY or SMTP_HOST / SMTP_PORT / SMTP_USER / SMTP_PASSWhere email goes out. Resend first, SMTP (for example Zoho ZeptoMail) otherwise.
RAZORPAY_KEY_ID / RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET / RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRETRazorpay. Start with test keys.
S3_BUCKET / S3_REGION / S3_ENDPOINT / S3_ACCESS_KEY_ID / S3_SECRET_ACCESS_KEYUploads in a bucket (S3, Cloudflare R2). Without these, uploads go to UPLOAD_DIR on disk.

Optional

GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID / _SECRET, MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID / _SECRETSign in with Google or Microsoft.
NEXT_PUBLIC_ROOT_DOMAINBase domain for organization subdomains.
NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL, NEXT_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_EMAIL, NEXT_PUBLIC_GRIEVANCE_OFFICER, NEXT_PUBLIC_LEGAL_ENTITY, NEXT_PUBLIC_LEGAL_JURISDICTIONShown on the legal pages. Built into the app.
SENTRY_DSN, ALERT_WEBHOOK_URLError alerts.
MAINTENANCE_MODE, MAINTENANCE_MESSAGEPut the site into maintenance.
CUSTOM_DOMAIN_CNAME_TARGET, CUSTOM_DOMAIN_IPSFor organizations using their own domain.
WEATHER_API_KEYThe weather module.
REDIS_URLShared rate limiting when you run more than one copy of the app.
